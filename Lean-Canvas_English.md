Lean Canvas: Ignis Solaris Studio
Game: Back to Thawing Valley
1. Problem
Psychological: The common pain points of "universal loneliness," "anxiety of efficiency," and the fear of falling behind (FOMO).
Mechanics: Many games lack the "freedom from obligation," forcing players into stressful, repetitive tasks.
Industry Gap: The industry often underrepresents and disrespects minority groups (e.g., non-binary individuals, ethnic minorities).
2. Customer Segments
Broad Market: Players of simulation, farming, and narrative-driven games.
Niche Target: Players who seek deep emotional connection, want to play at their own pace, and value genuine inclusivity in the games they play.
3. Unique Value Proposition (UVP)
A "psychological sanctuary grounded in psychology."
We provide "micro-healing" and an experience of being "unconditionally loved."
We offer "freedom from obligation" and "true inclusivity" as core design pillars.
4. Solution
Product: The game Back to Thawing Valley.
Core Loop: Empowers the player as a "Town Leader" with creative and community-building power (like Animal Crossing), rather than a "capitalist efficiency" loop (like Stardew Valley).
Core Experience: Driven by unique, deep NPC relationships (friendship/romance) and a compelling overarching story.
5. Channels
Early Community: Building an authentic developer narrative on platforms like Red Notes (Xiaohongshu); cross-promotion within indie developer communities.
Demo Distribution: Launching on Steam (to gather wishlists) and itch.io (to gather in-depth player feedback).
Amplification: Strategic outreach to YouTubers and Streamers for "let's play" content; targeted paid promotion during key launch windows.
6. Revenue Streams
Primary: A premium, buy-to-play title on PC and consoles.
Market Size: Low-spec hardware requirements to maximize the potential paid player base.
Longevity: Post-launch support for Steam Workshop (Mod Support) to encourage User-Generated Content (UGC) and extend the game's life.
Future: Potential for DLCs and IP licensing.
7. Cost Structure
Development (Variable): Art and music production (via outsourcing or revenue-sharing agreements).
Marketing (Variable): Paid promotion and advertising for demo and official launch periods.
Operations (Fixed): Business registration/taxes, website/domain hosting fees, software subscriptions.
8. Key Metrics
Market Validation: Steam Wishlist count and total demo downloads.
Product Success: Final sales units and the game's Steam review score (%).
Community Health: The quality and depth of player feedback, which indicates genuine engagement and anticipation for the full game.
9. Unfair Advantage
Founder's Expertise:
A psychology background ensures the authenticity of the "micro-healing" and "psychological sanctuary" concepts.
2 years of full-time QA experience ensures a high-quality, polished, and intuitive User Experience (UX).
Product Differentiation: A core player fantasy of being a "Town Leader" (community creation) versus a "Farmer" (financial efficiency).
Narrative-Driven Core: A deep, character-driven story in a traditionally mechanics-focused genre is the most difficult element for competitors to copy.
